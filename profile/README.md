### What we do

Most embodied AI is built to finish a task. We build it to be present with people.

The hard part is no longer motion — it is **judgment**: knowing when to yield, when to ask,
when to wait, and how to remain predictable when the world around it is not.

At the center is a **world model that includes people** — not just objects and obstacles,
but intentions, attention, and emotional state — so the system can predict how a moment will
unfold and choose the action a careful person would.
